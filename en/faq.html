Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The free version downloads up to 1080p with all basic features. Pro adds trim, GIFs, higher quality, more downloads at once and following channels. In this version Pro is given out by request.

Which sites work?

Many popular video, social and music sites, plus direct file links. Sites change often; the app updates its download engine by itself to keep up.

Why does a video say "not supported"?

Live streams, photo posts and DRM-protected content (for example paid streaming services) are not supported. The app never tries to get around protection.

macOS says the app "can't be opened"

Test builds are not signed with an Apple certificate yet. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Ducky Downloader. You only need to do this once.

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC"

Test builds are not signed yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only need to do this once.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works fully without one. An account is only needed for Pro.

Does the app collect data about me?

No. There is no tracking, no analytics and no ads. The app only contacts the sites you download from, update servers and, if you sign in, our account server.

Does it work without internet?

The app opens and shows your history and files without internet. Downloading needs a connection, of course.

How do updates work?

The app looks for a new version once a day and asks before installing. Updates are signed, and the signature is checked before anything is installed.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows: Settings → Apps → Ducky Downloader → Uninstall. macOS: drag Ducky Downloader from Applications to the Bin. Your downloaded files are never deleted. App settings stay unless you remove them (Windows asks during uninstall).

Where are my files?

In your Downloads folder unless you chose another one in the app. "Show in folder" in the history opens it.
