Content removal requests

Ducky Downloader is a desktop tool. It does not host, store or share any content: files are downloaded by users to their own computers from the original sites. We therefore cannot remove content from those sites.

If you believe your rights are infringed:

  • Contact the site that hosts the content; it can remove it at the source.
  • If your request is about Ducky Downloader itself (for example our website or app), write to hcorer@live.com with your name, the content concerned, the right you hold and how it is infringed.

We answer requests within a reasonable time.

Ducky Downloader is not affiliated with any website or platform.
