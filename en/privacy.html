Privacy notice

This notice explains what the Ducky Downloader desktop app does with personal data, under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Short version

  • The app has no telemetry, analytics, ads or trackers.
  • Your links, downloads, history, settings and site-account cookies stay on your computer.
  • You can use the app without an account.

Data kept on your device

  • Download queue and history, settings, file names and folders.
  • Site-account cookies, if you add a site account. They are filtered to the site you choose, encrypted, and the key is kept in your operating system's keychain.
  • A local log file for troubleshooting. Secrets are removed from it before it is written. You can also hide links in the log.

This data is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by removing the app's data folder.

What the app sends over the internet

  • Requests to the sites of the links you download, made on your behalf, the same way a browser would.
  • A check for engine updates: a plain request with no identifiers.
  • A check for app updates shortly after start and once a day: a plain request to GitHub, where our releases are hosted, with no identifiers. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website (GitHub's privacy statement applies). An update is downloaded only after you agree.
  • Only if you sign in: account and license requests to our server.

Diagnostics

"Export diagnostics" creates a zip file on your computer. It is never uploaded automatically. You decide whether to send it to us.

Data controller and your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 and GDPR Articles 15–22 you can ask what data we hold about you, ask for correction or deletion, object to processing and complain to the supervisory authority (in Türkiye: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu). Because the desktop app keeps your data only on your device, we usually hold none.

Ducky Downloader is not affiliated with any website or platform.
